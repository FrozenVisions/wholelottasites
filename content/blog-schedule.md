# Blog schedule

One post a day from 22 October to 19 November 2026 is in the first table. No two days in a row are the same group. The next table runs from 20 November to 25 December 2026. Five posts are inserted from 20 November, and the later rows move on by five days. Each keyword is unused by any post or earlier row. Each inserted post has one primary keyword, and that keyword includes the area.

To publish the post for a date, add a new file at `content/blog/<slug>.md`. The build prerenders `/blog/<slug>/`, adds it to the sitemap, and lists it on `/blog/`. You do not register the slug anywhere else. Do not publish a file before you mean it to go live: whatever is in `content/blog/` is public after the next deploy.

## File format

```markdown
---
title: The article H1
seoTitle: Optional document title | WholeLottaSites
description: One or two sentences for the meta description and the blog index.
date: YYYY-MM-DD
keyword: the target search, exactly as in the table below
slug: same-as-filename
---

Opening paragraph.

## A section

Paragraphs, lists and [links](/pricing/). Use ## and ### headings, bullet lists, numbered lists, **bold**, *italic* and links. Internal links can omit the trailing slash. UK English.

Do not invent statistics, reviews, ratings, client names or testimonials. Do not promise a Google ranking or say “#1”. If you mention a price, use only these figures: founding client offer, first 20 clients, £200 + VAT one-off (£240 inc VAT), or £50 a month inc VAT for 12 months (£600 total, £41.67 + VAT a month); first year of hosting included; first year of a standard low-cost domain included and registered in the client’s name; premium domains cost extra; from year two the client pays domain renewal and hosting, or monthly care; website fix typically £150–£400 after a free audit; East Midlands mini-fix £50 + VAT; Google Business Profile setup or fix £149 on its own (included with a founding website); optional Google management £99–£150 a month; optional website care from £25 a month after 3 free months on a founding website (Essential £25, Growth £50 with 4 SEO blog posts a month, Pro £75 with 8; posts help the site grow on Google over time, and are not a promise of rankings, traffic or leads); bigger projects from around £1,200. Do not publish promo code names. Proof sites you may name, with no results attached: wholelottavinyl.com, safeplate.org.uk, mcdecoratingni.co.uk and vinylbuyback.co.uk. The Sticky Service and WLV Outlet may be named as sites on the portfolio. WLV Outlet is in progress. No testimonials, traffic or rankings.
```

Write a useful article that answers the keyword. If a service page already targets a similar search, link to it and take a how-to angle. Do not paste the service page.
