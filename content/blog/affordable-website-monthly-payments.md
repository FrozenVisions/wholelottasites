## If a payment is missed

Monthly payments are taken automatically by card through Stripe, so there isn’t a monthly invoice to forget. A missed payment means a charge that didn’t go through: an expired card, a decline, or details that have changed.

If a monthly payment is missed, the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee or interest. The monthly total stays £600 inc VAT. Contact me and we’ll take that payment. Keep a working card in place until every agreed payment has been taken. A failed card is not a discount: you still pay the full price.
