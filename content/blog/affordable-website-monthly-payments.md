Affordable website monthly payments spread a fixed price. They are not a free website, and they are not a cheap first month that becomes a different bill. I’m Chris Faulkes, a web designer. The small-business website I build is £500, paid in full: in one go, or in instalments that add up to the same £500. The schedules are on the [pay monthly website](/pay-monthly-website/) page. This is what to check before you agree to one, including mine.

## Add up the total

Start with the total, not the monthly line. Write every payment down and add them. If the sum is higher than the price you were told, ask what the extra is before you start.

On the [pricing](/pricing/) page the small-business website is £500. These are the only ways I split it:

- **£150 up front, then £50 a month for 7 months.** £150 plus seven payments of £50 is £500.
- **£100 up front, then £100 a month for 4 months.** £100 plus four payments of £100 is £500.
- **Three payments of £166.67, £166.67 and £166.66.** The last payment is one penny less than the first two, so the three add up to £500.

Paying £500 at the start is the same website. I don’t add a setup fee or interest. A plan changes the dates, not the price.

While you pay in instalments, the domain and the hosting stay in my name, WholeLottaSites. They are not a separate bill on top of the £500 while those payments are still running. Once the £500 is paid, the domain transfers to you. A renewal after that is around £5–£10 a year. After payoff you can choose optional monthly care, typically £29–£50. It does not start while the instalments are still running. A bigger project is quoted from around £1,200.

Ask whose name is on the domain, and when it moves to you. On a pay-monthly plan here, the domain and the hosting stay in my name, WholeLottaSites, for as long as the instalments are running. I hold the account. The domain transfers to you only once the full £500 is paid. It does not transfer earlier.

The site is handed over once the £500 is paid. Until then you are paying the instalments on that price. A full handover is not a further bill. ## If a payment is missed

Instalments are taken automatically by card through Stripe, so there isn’t a monthly invoice to forget. A missed payment means a charge that didn’t go through: an expired card, a decline, or details that have changed.

If an instalment is missed, the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee or interest. The total stays £500. Contact me and we’ll take that instalment on the plan you picked. Keep a working card in place until every agreed payment has been taken. A failed card is not a discount: you still pay the full price.
