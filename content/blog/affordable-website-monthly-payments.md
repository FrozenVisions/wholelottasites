## If a payment is missed

Instalments are taken automatically by card through Stripe, so there isn’t a monthly invoice to forget. A missed payment means a charge that didn’t go through: an expired card, a decline, or details that have changed.

If an instalment is missed, the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee or interest. On a £500 instalment plan the total stays £500. On pay monthly the total stays £750. Pay monthly is a separate £750 price, not finance or credit. Contact me and we’ll take that instalment on the plan you picked. Keep a working card in place until every agreed payment has been taken. A failed card is not a discount: you still pay the full price.
