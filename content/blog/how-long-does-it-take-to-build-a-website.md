How long does it take to build a website? For a UK small business, a founding client launches within 7 days of receiving your content. We tweak until you’re happy before launch. The main thing that decides the date is getting the text, photos, logo, services and prices to the builder. I’m Chris Faulkes, a web designer in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire. Nothing here is guaranteed. There is no promise of a Google position, of rankings, or of leads.

## How long does it take to build a website?

A founding client launches within 7 days of receiving your content, and we tweak until you’re happy before launch. I won’t add a second number of days or weeks. The sequence is on [how it works](/how-it-works/).

### When the 7 days start

The 7 days start when the content is received. The form asks for your name, the business name, an email and a short note about what you do. A phone number and a current website are optional. You don’t need a finished brief to ask. A design for your business starts after the deposit. Before that, you look at sample sites.

### The text, photos, logo, services and prices

The pages need something true to say. Text: what you do, in words a customer would use, and how to get in touch. Services: the jobs you will actually do. Prices, where you are happy to show them. You do not have to print a figure you cannot keep. Photos of your work, your premises or your van, if you have them. A logo, if you already use one.

What to put on the pages is in [your first website for a new business](/blog/first-website-for-my-business/). A [website built from scratch](/website-built-from-scratch/) is designed and coded for the business, not from a shared theme. With no photos, the design starts from the words after the deposit.

### What can wait

A logo, photos, the towns you cover or your address and hours, and a domain you already own, all help, and they can wait.

## The steps on the page

[How it works](/how-it-works/) names four steps. Sample, Build and Launch are the same job in longer notes.

1. **Samples.** Look at live sites and example designs. That’s what you see before a deposit.
2. **Deposit.** A design for your business starts once the deposit is in. We agree the pages.
3. **Built to launch.** I build the full site from scratch. A founding client launches within 7 days of receiving the content.
4. **Go live.** We tweak until you’re happy before launch. Then I help you connect your domain.

The [portfolio](/portfolio/) is the sample: live sites and example designs, not a design made for your business. The build is the pages we agreed, phones first, fast and over HTTPS, with a title and description on every page and a form that reaches you. That is solid foundations, not a promise of a Google position or of leads.

## Before you ask how long

1. Can you send what you do, the services, and any prices you will publish?
2. Do you have photos, and a logo if you already use one?
3. Have you looked at sample sites? A design starts after the deposit.
4. Will the content be ready, so the 7 days can start?
5. If any answer is no, send the short note. Missing content moves the date.
