How much deposit for a website is worth asking before a plumber, a shop or a sole trader in Mansfield, Nottingham or the rest of the East Midlands pays. I’m Chris Faulkes, a web designer in Mansfield. A website deposit is money paid so the work can start, before the rest of the site is finished. Many designers ask for part up front. ## What a website deposit is for

A website deposit is the start of an agreed job, usually the homepage design, not a fee for holding a slot. Look at finished sites first. I don’t promise a place on Google, or a number of calls.

## How much deposit for a website: what to ask before you pay

I will not invent a figure. Ask for the deposit in pounds, including VAT, beside the total price.

The [pricing](/pricing/) page and the FAQ do not publish a separate deposit figure. [How it works](/how-it-works/) says:

- You pay the deposit to start. There are no free mock-ups for unpaid leads.
- Before a deposit, the sample is a site already on the [portfolio](/portfolio/).
- The deposit starts the homepage design.
- If you don’t like that design, and we can’t get it right after reasonable revisions, you get the deposit back in full, before any further work.
- Once you approve the homepage design, that refund no longer applies.

That is this site’s rule, not another designer’s contract, and it is not legal advice. Paying a deposit for a website in Mansfield or Nottinghamshire still needs it in writing. More questions are in [questions to ask a web designer in Mansfield](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-web-designer-mansfield/).

The rest of the site is built once you approve the homepage. A founding client launches within 7 days of receiving the content. “Soon” is not a timeline.

The domain should be in your name. The first year of hosting is included, and so is the first year of a standard low-cost domain, registered in your name. Premium domains cost extra. From year two you pay the domain renewal and hosting, or monthly care.

On pay monthly, the site stays on our hosting. After 12 payments, the site is yours. There is no exit fee. You can cancel the monthly payments with 30 days’ notice. If you stop early, you can either pay the remaining balance and take full ownership, or we take the site down and hand over your own text, photos and domain. The domain is always yours. You stay the owner of the Google Business Profile. I will not ask for that password.

## Red flags on a web designer deposit

A web designer deposit with a written total and a timeline is ordinary. Pause on a large sum up front with nothing written about what it covers, what you see next, or when a refund applies. Pause if there is no timeline, if the domain would sit in the designer’s name, or if the quote promises a ranking or a review written for you.

## A checklist before you pay

1. The deposit, in pounds including VAT, and the work it starts.
2. What you see before the balance, and a timeline you can check.
3. When a refund still applies, and when it ends.
4. The full total next to the deposit. Here that is £240 inc VAT once, or £600 inc VAT over 12 months. The monthly price is not finance or credit.
5. The domain in your name, and the year-two cost.
6. Who owns the site if you finish paying, and if you stop early.
7. No writing, no timeline, or a ranking promise: do not pay yet.
