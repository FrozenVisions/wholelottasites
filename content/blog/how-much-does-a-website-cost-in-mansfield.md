## What the Mansfield price includes

A small business in Mansfield, Sutton, Mansfield Woodhouse or the villages around them usually needs a short site, not a large one. The founding price is that site, built from scratch rather than dropped onto a theme other companies are already using.

Proper local keywords are written into the pages: what you do, and the places you cover, as real text. The layout is mobile-first, because most of those enquiries start on a phone. Pages are kept fast. There is HTTPS, a title and description on every page, a contact form, and your phone number in a form a phone can dial. If you want card payments, you set up your own Stripe account and I connect the links.

The first year of hosting is included. So is the first year of a standard low-cost domain, registered in your name. Premium domains cost extra. From year two you pay the domain renewal and hosting, or monthly care.

None of that is a promise of a Google position, a Maps ranking, or a number of leads. A clear site makes you easier to understand. Whether Google shows you, and whether someone rings, is not something I can guarantee.

## What it does not include

A large shop with stock and shipping, a web app, or a site with a great many pages is a different job. I quote those from around £1,200, and I say so before you pay if that is what you are describing.

If you already have a live site and only one thing is broken — a form, the mobile layout, speed, SSL or redirects, or the website link on your Google Business Profile — that can be the [£50 + VAT East Midlands mini-fix](/website-mini-fix/). It covers Nottinghamshire, including Mansfield, plus Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Lincolnshire and Northamptonshire. It is one fix, not a new website.

A wider rescue, after a free audit, is usually around £150–£400. If patching is a false economy, the honest job is a rebuild at the founding price, not a mini-fix stretched until it pretends to be one.

Google Business Profile setup on its own is £149. It is included with a founding-client website. You stay the Google owner. I don’t promise a position on Maps. Optional management after that is £99–£150 a month. Optional website care, after 3 months included with a founding site, is from £29 a month, usually £29–£50.

## A sensible way to compare quotes

When you are asking how much a website costs in Mansfield, write the total down, not only the first month. Ask whose name the domain is in. Ask what happens in year two. Ask whether the price is a finished site or a login to a builder you then have to learn. Ask what a missed card payment does. On my monthly plan the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee. The total stays £600 including VAT.

Then ask what the page will actually say. A Mansfield plumber, café or decorator does not need the same pages, but they do need the towns, the phone number and the job names in text a person can read.
