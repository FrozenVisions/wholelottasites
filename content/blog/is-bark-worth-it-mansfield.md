Is Bark worth it for a trade in Mansfield? I’m Chris Faulkes, a web designer in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire. I checked the public prices on bark.com on 26 November 2026. This note is those figures, and the alternative of a site you own plus a Google Business Profile. It is not a complaint about Bark, and it is not a promise that either route will fill the diary.

## Is Bark worth it Mansfield?

On 26 November 2026 the Bark Starter Pack was about £149 + VAT, which is £178.80, for roughly 100 credits. That is about £1.80 a credit. Credits expire after 3 months. You pay to contact leads. Paying does not mean they hire you. Bark does not guarantee that, and neither do I.

I have not listed every Bark product. The Starter Pack is the one I checked that day. If the page has moved since 26 November 2026, use the price on bark.com, not this paragraph.

A credit gets you the chance to contact someone who posted a job. It is not a booked job, and it is not a deposit. Some of those people will already have spoken to other trades. Some will not reply. That is the nature of a paid introduction. It can still be worth it if the jobs that do come back cover the credits before they expire. I can’t see your leads, so I won’t pretend to know the answer for your trade.

## What you are comparing it with

The other route is a place that stays yours. A website on a domain in your name, and a [Google Business Profile](/google-business-profile/) that uses the same name, phone number and towns. Someone in Mansfield, Mansfield Woodhouse or Sutton-in-Ashfield who already has your number, or who searches the job and the town, can read what you do and tap to call. A directory and a site can sit side by side. One does not cancel the other.

The site does not expire in 3 months. You still pay to have it built, and from year two you pay the domain and hosting, or care. Those figures are on [pricing](/pricing/). The founding client offer, for the first 20 clients, is £200 + VAT one-off (£240 inc VAT), or £50 a month inc VAT for 12 months (£600 total, £41.67 + VAT a month). The monthly price is not finance or credit. I don’t promise a Google position or a number of calls.

Pay monthly includes 12 months of Essential care, normally £25 a month, for as long as those payments run. After the 12 months the site is yours. You can keep care at Essential £25, Growth £50 or Pro £75 a month, or £250, £500 or £750 a year (pay 10 months, get 12), or leave. On any care plan, changes are made within 3 working days, and you get a short plain-English note on what was done. Paying once, care is free for 3 months, then optional. Google Business Profile setup is included with a founding website. On its own it is £149.

If the question is only “is Bark worth it Mansfield?” for the next three months of credits, the Starter Pack price above is the number I can stand behind. If the question is where a customer should find you next year, that is the website.

## A short way to decide

1. Take the Starter Pack figure you can see today. On 26 November 2026 that was about £178.80 including VAT for roughly 100 credits, about £1.80 each, gone after 3 months.
2. Ask whether the jobs you actually win from those contacts cover that spend. Only you can see that.
3. If you also want an address that does not expire with the credits, look at [pricing](/pricing/) and send a brief on [start your site](/start/).
