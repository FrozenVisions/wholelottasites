A pay monthly website is easy to misunderstand. Some offers are a cheap first month that becomes a different bill. Some are a subscription you cannot see the end of. Some are finance, with a total that is never written down. This is what the monthly option on my site actually is, for a business in Nottingham or anywhere else I work. I am based in Mansfield, not in an agency in the city centre. The work is the same either way.

## The monthly price, written as a total

Pay monthly is £50 a month including VAT for 12 months. Twelve payments are £600 including VAT. Each month is £41.67 plus VAT. Paying once is £200 + VAT, which is £240 including VAT. The monthly route costs more in total. That difference is the price of spreading it. It is not interest added on top of £240, and it is not a credit agreement.

Those £50 payments buy the website. They are not monthly care. Care, after 3 months included with a founding-client website, is optional and starts from £29 a month.

The offer is for the first 20 clients. I am building up reviews, so those first 20 get founding pricing. In return I ask for an honest Google review and permission to show the finished site in the portfolio. I don’t publish how many places are left, and I don’t invent reviews to fill the gap. The page that sets this out is the [founding client offer](/founding-offer/). The [pay monthly website](/pay-monthly-website/) page uses the same figures.

## What you get for that money

You get one small-business site, built from scratch. Not a theme shared with a row of other Nottingham businesses. The pages say what you do and where you work, in real text, with local keywords for those services and towns. That is the local SEO. It is wording and structure. It is not a promise that you will rank, and not a promise of leads.

The site is laid out for a phone first, kept fast, and served over HTTPS. Every page has a title and description. There is a contact form and a phone number people can tap. If you take card payments, that is your own Stripe account, which I connect.

The first year of hosting is included. The first year of a standard low-cost domain is included, and the domain is registered in your name. I help you point it at the site. I don’t need to own it. Premium domains cost extra. From year two you pay the domain renewal and hosting, or monthly care.

A founding client launches within 7 days of receiving the content. We tweak the agreed site until you’re happy before it goes live. That is revisions of the site we agreed, not a second, larger project slipped in at the end.

## What people in Nottingham often think is included

A full online shop with stock levels and shipping is not this price. A booking system you want built from nothing is not this price. A site for a group with many branches is not this price. Those are quoted from around £1,200, and I will say so before any money changes hands.

A Google Business Profile is included with the founding website: setup, normally £149 on its own. You stay the owner. I am added as a manager. I will not tell you that you will be first on Maps. If you only want the listing, and you already have a site, the £149 price still applies.

If the site you have is already live and only one concrete thing is wrong, the [£50 + VAT mini-fix](/website-mini-fix/) may be the job instead. It covers Nottinghamshire, Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Lincolnshire and Northamptonshire. It is not a new website and not a redesign.

## How the payments work

You choose monthly or one-off when you decide to go ahead. Monthly payments are taken automatically by card through Stripe. There isn’t a separate invoice to remember. If a payment fails, the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee or interest. On the monthly plan the total stays £600 including VAT. A failed card is not a discount.

Before a deposit, you look at sample sites. A design made for your business starts after the deposit, not before. The sequence is on [how it works](/how-it-works/).

## After the twelfth payment

When the website price is paid, the build payments stop. You can take a full handover, or continue with optional care from £29 a month. Care covers uptime and SSL checks, small edits, a check that the form still arrives, and a nudge before the domain renews. It is a choice. From year two you also pay the domain renewal, and hosting if you are not on care.
