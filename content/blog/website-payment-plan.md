A website payment plan is a fixed price split into payments you can add up. It is not a subscription that never ends, and it is not finance. I’m Chris Faulkes, a web designer in Mansfield. The monthly line sticks. The total is what you are agreeing to.

The plan most people mean is the founding client offer for a new small-business website. Pay monthly is the main way to spread it: £50 a month including VAT for 12 months. Twelve payments are £600 including VAT. Each month is £41.67 + VAT. Paying once is £200 + VAT, which is £240 including VAT. The monthly total is higher because the price is spread. It is not interest on top of £240, and it is not a credit agreement.

It’s for the first 20 clients. I’m building up reviews, so those first 20 get this price, in return for an honest Google review and permission to show the site in the portfolio once it’s live. ## Which bill you are looking at

“Payment plan” gets used for jobs that are not a plan. I quote these separately.

- **A new small-business website.** £50 a month inc VAT for 12 months, or £200 + VAT one-off. The pages are the same either way. Those £50 payments buy the site. They are not the care plan.
- **One concrete fix on a live site.** The East Midlands [website mini-fix](/website-mini-fix/) is £50 + VAT (£60 including VAT). We agree that one fix before I start. It is a single job, not twelve payments.
- **A wider rescue.** After a free audit that lists 3–5 issues, a typical fix is usually around £150–£400. If patching will not hold, the rebuild is the founding price above.
- **Google Business Profile.** Setup or a fix is £149 on its own, and it is included with a founding website. Optional management is £99–£150 a month. You stay the owner. I don’t promise a place on Maps.
- **Website care.** Optional, £29–£50 a month. A founding website includes 3 months free, then you choose care or a full handover.
- **A bigger project.** A full shop with stock and shipping, a booking system built from nothing, or many more pages, is quoted from around £1,200. I say so before any money changes hands.

The £50 + VAT mini-fix and the £50-a-month website payment share a number and nothing else.

Local keywords for the work and the towns are written in. That is not a promise of a Google position or of leads. ## How the year runs

You choose monthly or one-off when you decide to go ahead. A design for your business starts after the deposit. Before that, the sample is a site already on the [portfolio](/portfolio/). The sequence is on [how it works](/how-it-works/).

Monthly payments are taken automatically by card through Stripe. There isn’t a monthly invoice to forget. If a charge doesn’t go through, the site is paused until that payment is caught up. I don’t add a late fee or interest. The total stays £600 including VAT.

The site is handed over once the website price is paid, whether that is the £240 or the twelfth £50. A full handover is not a further bill. Care after the free months is separate: uptime and SSL checks, small edits, a form check, and a nudge before the domain renews. It does not carry on the £50 website payments.

## When you do not need a plan

If the site is already live and only one thing is wrong — a form, the phone layout, speed, SSL or redirects, or a missing website link on the Google listing — that is the £50 + VAT mini-fix. It covers Nottinghamshire, including Mansfield, plus Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Lincolnshire and Northamptonshire. Several faults are a rescue, usually around £150–£400 after the free audit on the [website redesign](/website-redesign/) page, or a rebuild on the plan above if the old site will not hold. I say which before you pay. [Google Business Profile](/google-business-profile/) setup stays £149 on its own.
